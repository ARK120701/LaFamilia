Diabetes Prevention Program

Structured support to manage and prevent diabetes.

Our Services

Health Guidance You Can Rely On

Our Diabetes Prevention Program combines nutrition education, lifestyle coaching, and regular health monitoring to help participants manage existing diabetes or reduce their risk in a supportive, familiar setting. Members learn practical ways to think about meals, movement, and daily habits, with our health team on hand to answer questions and check in on progress. Because everyone's needs are different, guidance is tailored to each participant rather than treated as one-size-fits-all. Alongside the individual attention, participants also find encouragement in the group — sharing meals, swapping tips, and celebrating small wins together. Our goal is to help build habits that feel sustainable, not overwhelming, so participants can carry them into everyday life. Every step of the way, our staff is here to listen, guide, and support the people we care for like family.

  • ✓ Nutrition education
  • ✓ Regular health monitoring
  • ✓ One-on-one coaching support
