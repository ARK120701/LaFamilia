Our Services & Programs

A full circle of care, every day of the week.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Adult Day Care Services

Every service is tailored to each participant's needs through an individualized care plan.

Personal Care Assistance

Support with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and mobility — delivered with patience, respect, and dignity by trained caregivers.

Nutritious Meals & Snacks

Fresh, home-style breakfast, lunch, and snacks made daily on-site, with menus that accommodate diabetic, low-sodium, and other dietary needs.

Recreational & Social Activities

Music therapy, arts and crafts, group games, gentle exercise, cultural celebrations, and outings that keep participants active and engaged.

Health & Wellness Monitoring

Vital sign checks, medication reminders, and coordination with physicians and families, overseen by our licensed nursing staff.

Door-to-Door Transportation

Safe, accessible transportation to and from the center, so families never have to worry about the daily commute.

Social & Emotional Support

A warm, welcoming community that reduces isolation, encourages friendship, and supports emotional well-being.

Care Plan Coordination

Individualized care plans developed with families and healthcare providers, reviewed and updated regularly.

Respite Care for Families

Flexible daytime care that gives family caregivers the time they need to work, rest, and recharge.

Bilingual, Culturally-Rooted Care

Our staff proudly serve our community in English and Spanish, honoring the traditions and comfort of home.
