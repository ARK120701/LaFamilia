Safe, reliable transportation to and from the center.

Our Services

Getting Here Is One Less Worry

Getting here shouldn't be a barrier to care. Our door-to-door pickup and drop-off service picks participants up from home each morning and returns them safely each afternoon, with trained drivers and accessible vehicles. Every driver is carefully vetted and trained to assist with mobility needs, so families can trust that their loved one is in caring, capable hands from the moment they step outside. Our vehicles are equipped to accommodate wheelchairs and walkers, making the ride as comfortable and dignified as the care that follows it. We know that dependable transportation is often the deciding factor in whether a family can access care at all, so we treat punctuality and safety as top priorities, not afterthoughts. Drivers get to know each participant and their routine, turning the daily commute into a familiar, reassuring part of the day. By handling the ride, we free families from the stress of coordinating rides or rearranging work schedules, giving them one less worry each morning and afternoon.

  • ✓ Door-to-door service
  • ✓ Accessible, wheelchair-friendly vehicles
  • ✓ Consistent daily schedule
