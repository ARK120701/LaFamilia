Physical Therapy & Gym

Guided exercise to maintain strength and independence.

Our Services

Building Strength, Safely

Our on-site gym gives participants a warm, familiar place to stay active without ever having to leave the building. Guided physical therapy sessions focus on maintaining mobility, rebuilding strength, and reducing the risk of falls, so participants can keep doing the everyday things that matter to them with greater confidence and independence. Trained staff are with participants every step of the way, watching closely and offering steady support so each movement feels safe rather than stressful. Because every body is different, every plan is different too — sessions are shaped around each participant's own ability, pace, and goals, and adjusted over time as those needs change. It's gentle, encouraging work, built on patience rather than pressure. For families, it's the reassurance that a loved one is staying strong and steady in caring, capable hands.

  • ✓ Individualized exercise plans
  • ✓ Low-impact strength equipment
  • ✓ Coordinated with physician recommendations
