Our Story

Built On Family, Rooted In Community

La Familia Adult Day Care Center was founded with a simple belief: every adult deserves a place where they feel safe, valued, and connected — and every family deserves peace of mind. What started as a small community effort has grown into a trusted home away from home for adults who need daytime care and companionship.

Our name says it all — La Familia, "the family." We treat every participant and every family member who walks through our doors as an extension of our own. Whether it's a warm meal, a helping hand, or simply someone to talk to, we're here for the whole journey.
