Art Workshop

Painting, crafting, and creative self-expression.

Our Services

A Space to Create

Our art workshop gives participants a relaxed space to paint, draw, and create — no experience necessary. Each week brings a new project, from watercolor and acrylic painting to drawing, collage, and simple crafts, so there's always something fresh to look forward to. Staff guide the group at an easy, unhurried pace, offering as much help or as little as each participant wants, because the point is enjoying the process, not getting it "right." Working with color, texture, and shape keeps hands and minds active and often sparks conversation and connection among participants. All materials are provided, and every finished piece is treated with care — framed or hung proudly around the center for family and friends to admire. It's a simple joy that gives participants something to feel genuinely proud of.

  • ✓ All materials provided
  • ✓ Work displayed at the center
