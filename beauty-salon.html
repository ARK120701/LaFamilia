Beauty Salon

Personal grooming that helps every participant look and feel their best.

Our Services

A Little Pampering, Right On-Site

Our on-site beauty salon offers participants hairstyling, manicures, and other personal grooming services in a relaxed, friendly setting. We know that feeling well-groomed can lift anyone's mood and confidence, and many of our members look forward to their salon appointment as a special part of the week. Our staff takes the time needed with each participant, accommodating individual preferences and any mobility or sensitivity needs. Whether it's a haircut, a festive style, or a simple manicure, we make sure every salon visit feels like an act of care, not just a service.

  • ✓ Hairstyling & haircuts
  • ✓ Manicures & personal grooming
  • ✓ Care tailored to each participant
