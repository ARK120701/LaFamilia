Social Services

Compassionate support for participants and their families, beyond day-to-day care.

Our Services

Personalized Support When You Need It Most

Our social services team works closely with participants and their families to identify needs, work through challenges, and connect with the right community resources. Whether it's helping navigate benefits eligibility, coordinating with healthcare providers, or simply offering a listening ear, we're here to ease some of the weight that comes with caring for a loved one. Every family's situation is different, so our approach is individual, practical, and always confidential. We believe caring well for a participant also means caring for the people walking alongside them, which is why our staff is available to talk, guide, and support families every step of the way.

  • ✓ Confidential one-on-one support
  • ✓ Connections to community resources
  • ✓ Guidance for families
