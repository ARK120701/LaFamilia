Indoor Games

Cards, board games, and friendly competition every day.

Our Services

A Little Friendly Competition

Rainy day or not, there's always a game going on at La Familia. From classic card games to bingo, dominoes, and board games, our indoor games program gives participants a fun, low-pressure way to stay mentally sharp and socially engaged. Tables fill up throughout the day with familiar favorites alongside puzzles and board games that welcome newcomers and longtime players alike, and our staff are always close by to explain the rules, adapt a game to a participant's abilities, or simply cheer on a good move. We build in friendly tournaments and themed bingo afternoons to give the week a little extra spark, with small prizes and plenty of laughter along the way. Whether someone wants to sharpen their card-counting skills, catch up on neighborhood news over dominoes, or just enjoy the company at the table, there's a seat waiting for them. No experience is necessary and every skill level is welcome, because at La Familia the real win is the time spent together.
